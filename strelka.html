The problem

Strelka Institute is a non-profit international project that educates the next generation of architects, designers and media professionals. It needed one web platform for all its projects and activities, and a new online product that would open its unique knowledge base to everyone.

My role

I worked in the institute’s design team under art director Anna Kulachek, together with the management and producers. With the team, I worked on the new platform and its design language. My main focus was the online education platform: I designed it from the first research and customer journey map to prototypes, final design and support during development.

1. One platform for all the institute’s projects

Together with the design team, I worked on a platform that brings all the institute’s projects and activities into one place: programmes, courses, the library, the events calendar and more.

We created a new architecture and user journeys, and a visual language that reinterprets the institute’s signature style in a fresh, modern way. The system is fully flexible and adapts to every screen size, with a user-friendly mobile version.

2. Courses built on Strelka’s knowledge

The core of the education project was interactive courses based on the institute’s knowledge base, free and open to everyone.

We started with brainstorming sessions. I drew the first customer journey map, studied existing education platforms and ran internal studies on how people learn online and use these interfaces. Then I built the prototypes that became the base of the final design.

I worked closely with the producers, who wrote the course scenarios and content, and later with the web developers to bring the product to life.

3. A first step towards a learning community

The next stage was an MVP of a social layer: a discussion where students talk to curators and experts. It launched as an early version, laying the groundwork for the platform’s growth.

Outcome

• The institute launched its new platform, which brought together its projects, from the architecture bureau to the education platform.
• The online education platform launched with interactive courses open to everyone.
• A new architecture, user journeys and visual language, with a responsive system for every screen size.
• In 2022 Strelka Institute stopped its activities as a non-profit educational organisation, and the platform closed with it.
