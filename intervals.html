The problem

Intervals is a media art festival with 17 indoor and outdoor installations, performances, a music event, a talk series and an education programme, spread across several locations in the city. It needed one bold identity that works at every touchpoint, from the website to posters, navigation and merch.

My role

I was part of the design team that created the festival’s visual language and overall design. My main tasks were the festival website and the key print materials: posters and the main brochure. With the team, I also worked on advertising, communication, navigation and merchandise.

1. A map of every touchpoint

We developed the visual language together with the producers, discussing the festival’s concept, philosophy and vision. In this phase we mapped all audience touchpoints and set up a detailed design workflow. Responsibilities were split across the team, while key decisions were made together to keep everything consistent.

2. A website to plan your route

The website brings together the programme, the festival map and practical information, so visitors can choose events and combine them with a walk around the city.

3. Posters, print and navigation

For the city, I designed the posters and the main brochure with the programme and the festival map. With the team, we also made navigation, covers for talks and education events, social media stories, press materials and merch, and supported every physical piece from concept to final production.

Outcome

• The city’s largest media art festival, with events and installations open to the public across several locations.
• One identity across every audience touchpoint, from the website to print, navigation and merch.
• The bold design language added to the public buzz around the event, and the festival keeps holding large-scale events every year.
